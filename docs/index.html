    Stay safe from scheme scams

    • No real scheme asks you to pay money to "approve" or "release" your benefit.
    • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or bank password with anyone, even an "officer".
    • Apply only on .gov.in / .nic.in websites, at a CSC, bank, post office or panchayat.
    • Report fraud on 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
